About Vegan Hypertrophy

Science-backed vegan muscle building, made practical

We help people build muscle on a vegan diet with clear training and nutrition frameworks that reduce guesswork.

Mission

Vegan Hypertrophy exists to make evidence-informed muscle-building guidance accessible for people following a plant-based diet.

The goal is simple: teach you how to train and eat in a way that is sustainable, measurable, and effective over the long term.

Philosophy

  • Principles over noise: We prioritize fundamentals that work consistently.
  • Progress over perfection: Small improvements, repeated weekly, compound into meaningful results.
  • Data over hype: Recommendations should be practical and informed by evidence, not trends.

Who this site is for

  • Beginners who want an organized starting point.
  • Vegans trying to build or preserve muscle with confidence.
  • People navigating bulk, cut, or recomp phases who want a calmer, more structured process.
