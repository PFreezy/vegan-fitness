If you are new, follow this order to build a strong foundation before diving into details.

Recommended path

  1. Step 1: Understand the mission

    Read Declaration of Intent to understand the guiding approach and intended audience.

  2. Step 2: Learn the core framework

    Read The Holy Trinity for the core pillars: training, nutrition, and recovery.

  3. Step 3: Set your baseline

    Use Status Quo and Setting Goals to define measurable starting metrics.

  4. Step 4: Build calorie strategy

    Go through the Calories In and Calories Out sections to align intake and output with your goal phase.

How to use this site effectively

  • Apply one concept at a time and track your response for 2–4 weeks.
  • Prioritize consistency in training and nutrition before optimizing small variables.
  • Use the free starter guide on the homepage as your quick-reference checklist.
